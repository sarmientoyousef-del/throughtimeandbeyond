Ideal flight · no air resistance

For a level launch and landing, horizontal and vertical motion separate:

x(t) = v cos θ · t
y(t) = v sin θ · t − ½gt²
T = 2v sin θ / g
R = v² sin(2θ) / g
H = v² sin²θ / (2g)

Substitute the shot’s speed and angle to see the ideal range.

With air resistance · numerical model

Drag pushes against the moving projectile and grows with its speed:

a⃗ = −gĵ − k|v⃗|v⃗
k = ρ CdA / (2m)
vₙ₊₁ = vₙ + aₙΔt
rₙ₊₁ = rₙ + vₙ₊₁Δt

The path is stepped forward in small time intervals until it reaches the terrain.
